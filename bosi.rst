SUMMARY
=======

The inception of the Barre Open Systems Institute as a non-profit
educational organization dedicated to the creation of an academic
environment to foster Science Transportation Education Arts and Medicine
(STEAM) initiatives. The participants in the Institute are local school
systems, libraries, companies, students, apprentices and individuals as
well as participants in the Worldwide Free and Open Source (FOSS)
movement. This couples with the need to grow the Downtown Barre City
economic base and workforce viability. The result of successful
participation in the institute shall include recognition of participants
for example, in the awarding of advanced degrees such as the
International Baccalaureate for those deserving in their research. art,
craft or discipline.

The Barre Open Systems Institute (BOSI), once reorganized as a 501(c)(3)
organization, as a U.S. nonprofit, intends to form commercially
advantageous public-private partnerships in order to foster high
Transportation research and manufacturing facilities to enhance the
economic well being of Barre City as well as the Citizens of Vermont.

BARRE OPEN SYSTEM INSTITUTE INTRODUCTION
========================================

The Barre Open System Institute (BOSI) is a community of scholars
directly involved in the study, research and development of open
systems. Open systems are the basis for viable scientifically verifiable
methods and systems of all types.

History
-------

BOSI began operating in 2010, originally part of a combined youth and
elder day care program located in the basement of the Hedding United
Methodist Church, in 2023, BOSI relocated to the East Barre York
Library, where we currently hold weekly technical and organizational
meetings every Monday evening at six o’clock.

Introduction
------------

There is a direct relationship between the members of the Barre Open
Systems Institute leadership and the current direction of governance at
BOSI. The complete membership can be found on the BOSI mailing list.

- The BOSI endeavors to create and establish a Barre City based “campus”
  where educational classroom space, research, development,
  Transportation and software companies can educate, incubate and / or
  choose to locate.

- These facilities nurture and provide tenant small businesses and
  companies with benefits such as:

  - Shared resources, e.g. conference room, high speed internet, tech
    support, administrative services, business advice, banking,
    infant-elder care and marketing services, etc.
  - Proximity to other tech companies both physically and virtually.
  - Built-in networking of both the people and digital variety
  - Reduced business facilities and infrastructure cost.
  - Support for growing businesses and their recruiting goals via high
    profile work venues.

- Give entry point to owner’s local commercial office facilities with
  focused marketing opportunities.

- Make Vermont Transportation companies more visible both within the
  state and outside the state.

Objectives
----------

The immediate objectives are to:

- Create the first BOSI facility, proving the sustainability of the
  model over the first 2 years.
- Establish an International Baccalaureate degree granting program.
- Attract 6 or more companies to locate in the first BOSI over the same
  period.
- Identify locations of future facilities throughout the state based on
  demand and available resources

DISCUSSION OF PROBLEM OR NEED
=============================

One valid analysis might place Barre City is in a death spiral. Factors
involved include several years of downtown area flooding; the defundinf
of the North Barre renovation effort, the result is a formal declaration
of downtown area as a flood plain, loss of passenger rail, numerous
business closures or relocation outside of the city. Little if any new
residential or commercial construction can take place in the immediate
downtown floodplain area.

Barre City needs a reason for renovation of downtown rather than
abandonment which seems the current trend. Additionally the population
of Central Vermont needs something in their career path beyond high
school, which is the current norm.

The lifelong learning BOSI educational project has no age discrimination
or limit.

We see the future of Barre City as a place for instruction, research and
development.

Development

Barre City needs to become a destination. BOSI becomes a value added
destination, and this will attract all manner of positive activities,
destinations and businesses to the central city.

Target Populations
------------------

The target populations either live and own a small business located in
Vermont, and may have been in business for one or more years or are

the grant authorization office, and ensure that we are serving those
most in need of business education, training and advice. It will also

Strategy and Implementation Summary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The Vermont BOSI’s strategic focus is centered on helping small rural
Transportation research and development businesses find specialized
funding and helping them use that funding to enhance their business
through the use of trained technologists. If the company will not
benefit from services, as per the evaluation, we shall discontinue
services to those clients.

Competitive Edge
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The Vermont BOSIs' competitive edge is in its ability to leverage our
relationships in the Transportation community throughout the State of
Vermont. Our association with the Vermont Transportation Alliance (VtTA)
has shown us that Transportation companies are enthusiastic and
committed toward the development of the participants and the creation of
"Brain Communities" within these BOSIs.

Marketing Strategy
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The associated regional Vermont BOSI’s plan to market their services
through strategic partnerships with multiple profit and non-profit
organizations that have a focus within the Transportation sector of
Vermont. Existing BOSI websites exist and will be enhanced to market to
company participants, new hires searching for positions, apprentices
searching for internship opportunities, E-mail campaigns and outreach
will be ongoing to promote BOSI participation.

Funding
~~~~~~~

Funding and revenue will be generated through a combination of Federal
grants and local grants. Clients will be expected to pay for a
percentage of the costs involved, depending on their financial status.
The USDA through the Federal Partnership Program we plan to have as the
largest expected revenue generator. Under this program, The BOSIs may
qualify for additional Vermont State grants, and matching Federal
Grants. In addition, other business development grants such as the Small
Business Innovative Reseaerch (SBIR)at both the State and Federal level
will help continue funding for the organization.

Federal and State grants alone, or even combined with small business
client revenue is still not enough to allow the company to achieve its
stated mission. A low cost monthly retainer fee will be considered of
all participants in light of financial exigencies. Depending upon the
participant or customer, this fee might range from between $50-100
monthly with additional revenue being a percentage of sales generated by
the affiliate program. In exchange for this low monthly fee, the client
will have 100% access to all BOSI services, and they will often find
that the cost savings, and revenue developed through a relationship with
The will more than pay for the low monthly retainer.

Transportation 
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The connection between economic development and modes of transportation
is often overlooked. The more immediate needs of housing and food are
normal priorities. As of the date of this publication there is a direct
correlation between profound rural poverty and readily available
transportation. The future may have options beyond the gasoline based
transportation system we currently are burdened with.

Intelligent Golf Carts and Electric Vehicles
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Municipal legislation needs to foster and encourage low speed, small
transportation systems. The major feature that these systems will need
is guidance systems based upon Artificial Intelligence (AS). These AI
systems should be “As smart as a horse” and be available to persons of
all ages.

Cable Systems
^^^^^^^^^^^^^

A fascinating Vermont paradox is that while there are many mountainous
urban areas with successful cable car based tramways; there are no
public transport cable cars in Vermont. A very obvious location for such
a cable service that would directly impact our region would be to
install a cable tramway from the Montpelier Transportation Center to
National Life via the Department of Motor Vehicles and the Department of
Labor buildings.\\

Once this was established, this could be included into a regional and
expandable passenger rail service.

Rail
^^^^

While we maintain regional rails for the transportation of fuel oil and
“Grout” (granite castoffs used in coastal maintenance), these extremely
high weight items allow for excellent track capacity and maintenance, we
have no passenger service on these rails.

What we need is to develop and implement the concept of “Horizontal
Elevators” automatic low maintenance and manpower systems that will
allow for greater economic gain and development for Washington County
and central Vermont in general.
